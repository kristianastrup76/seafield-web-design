Questions you might have

If this is your first time getting a website built, here's what people usually ask first.

Do I need to buy my own domain name, or do you do that? +

We handle it for you. Domain registration is included as part of your website package — we register a fitting .co.uk (or .com) address for your business and point it at your new site, so it's one less thing you need to sort out yourself.

Do I get a professional email address (e.g. info@mybusiness.co.uk)? +

Yes — we can set up a professional email address on your own domain (like info@yourbusiness.co.uk) alongside your website, so you're not stuck sending business emails from a personal Gmail or Hotmail address.

What happens if I want to update text or photos later? +

Every package includes one round of small text and photo swaps after launch, at no extra cost. Beyond that — a bigger redesign, new pages, that sort of thing — we're happy to help, billed simply by the hour rather than another fixed fee.

Who owns the website? +

You do — outright. The domain, the design, the content and the hosting account are all yours. The £18/month hosting fee keeps everything online, secure and backed up, but the site itself belongs to you, not us.
